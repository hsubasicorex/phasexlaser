MAGIS DI imaging systems in a flexo prepress room

A MAGIS DI is a drum imager. Film or plate is held on a rotating drum under vacuum, an imaging head writes along the drum, and the result depends on vacuum hold, drum runout, head focus and power, axis registration, cooling and extraction working together. A complaint that arrives as “the imager is soft” is usually a question about which of those has drifted.

ECO3 is the manufacturer and maintains its own technical service in North America. We do not present ourselves as a replacement for that. Plants come to us for a second opinion, for cover when their own schedule and the OEM schedule do not meet, or for component-level work on hardware that sits around the imaging chain.

Scope in one paragraph

PhaseX can evaluate drum drive and bearings, vacuum generation and sealing, accessible imaging-head interfaces and external optics, linear and rotary motion with its feedback, industrial controls and I/O, interlocks, cooling and extraction. We do not open a sealed imaging head or laser module, we do not supply or alter Amfortis, RIP or material recipes, we do not select plate or film material, and we do not accept a plate process or release production. Software licences and OEM updates stay with the customer and ECO3.

Observable machine symptoms

  • Imaging starts and aborts, or the machine will not reach ready
  • Vacuum will not hold, or hold varies across the drum
  • Soft, uneven or shifted imaging across the format
  • Drum speed, registration or axis reference faults
  • Head not available, unstable indication, or communication loss
  • Cooling, flow, temperature or extraction alarm
  • Cycle completes but output has changed against the plant's own reference

A symptom routes the evaluation; it does not diagnose a failed part.

Access, safety and customer-owned decisions

Send alarm screens, nameplates and the configuration page. Do not send Amfortis or RIP recipes, calibration files, software licence keys or customer artwork.

Material selection, plate process acceptance and production release stay with the plant. Where a module is ECO3-only, we say so and route it rather than opening it.

What to send before service planning

  • Machine model and serial number as shown on the plate
  • Imaging head generation and installed options
  • Drum width, vacuum arrangement and extraction package
  • Exact alarm text and the first observable change
  • Whether the machine is stopped, degraded or in planned maintenance
  • Site access contact and available service window

Questions owners ask

Is PhaseX an authorised ECO3 service provider?

No. ECO3 has its own technical service organisation in North America, and we are not part of it and hold no ECO3 appointment. We are independent capacity a plant can choose, and we say that in writing before any work.

Will you open the imaging head?

No. A sealed imaging head or laser module is not opened. We establish whether the fault sits in that module or in the drum, vacuum, motion, control or utility chain around it, and route an OEM-only module accordingly.

Can you supply or correct a MAGIS DI imaging recipe?

No. Amfortis, RIP and material recipes, calibrated material databases and software licences stay with the plant and ECO3. We work on machine hardware and report measured machine condition.

What does a first reply need?

The model and serial number from the plate, the head generation, the drum and vacuum arrangement, the exact alarm text, and what changed first. That is normally enough to say whether a site visit is the right next step.
