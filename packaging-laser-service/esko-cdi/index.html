The CDI platform, identified before work begins

CDI is Esko’s family of digital flexographic plate imagers. The machine model, generation, imaging configuration, control environment and surrounding prepress workflow vary across the installed base. PhaseX starts with the nameplate, installed options, available records and the symptom reported by the operator.

A plate result or machine alarm is an observation, not a diagnosis. The written evaluation scope states which accessible machine systems will be assessed and which proprietary, sealed or process-validation work remains outside that scope.

CDI platform families and service boundary

Esko publishes CDI Spark, CDI Advance and CDI Crystal as distinct platform families with model- and generation-specific configurations. PhaseX confirms the exact machine, installed options, documentation, access and safety conditions before accepting a service scope.

PhaseX has completed on-site service work on Esko CDI flexo imagers. That experience supports equipment identification and service planning; it is not a claim of Esko authorization, factory certification or a guaranteed repair outcome.

Installed imaging options and workflow software are recorded as part of the machine configuration. PhaseX works only with customer-licensed software and customer-authorized access. In a CDI Crystal XPS line, CDI Crystal is the laser imager and XPS Crystal is the UV LED exposure unit; the service boundary for each unit is confirmed separately. An XPS request may be limited to accessible electrical and control systems.

Independent status

PhaseX is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorized by Esko. Esko, CDI, Spark, Advance, Crystal and XPS identify customer-owned equipment and workflows only.

Why a model-specific independent scope matters

A CDI imager combines imaging, motion, control, cooling and workflow dependencies. Similar operator reports can originate in different parts of the machine or in a separate production stage. The service scope therefore follows the exact platform, installed configuration and production impact.

Independent service can be useful for out-of-production equipment, a second technical assessment or a clearly separated non-warranty scope. Active warranty and manufacturer agreements are reviewed before work is accepted.

Describe what the operator can observe

Useful starting information includes a job that will not begin, a new image-quality change, a motion or control alarm, an intermittent stop, unusual sound or vibration, or a condition that changes as the machine runs. Include when the change began and whether it affects every job or only certain work.

These observations help define the evaluation; none identifies a failed part by itself. The wider flexo plate imaging page separates imaging, exposure and processing equipment, while the symptom index routes reports that belong to another equipment class.

What we accept

Customer-owned Esko CDI platforms may be accepted for on-site evaluation and service across the United States and Canada, including out-of-production equipment. Feasibility depends on the exact model, configuration, condition, records, access, safety requirements and parts availability.

PhaseX is an independent service company. We are not an authorized Esko service center, agent, representative or affiliate, and we do not perform Esko warranty work. Equipment under an active warranty or manufacturer agreement is accepted only within a scope authorized in writing by the appropriate party.

No specific repair is promised before evaluation. PhaseX documents the as-found condition, the approved work and the measured as-left condition; original manufacturer certification is not claimed where the required acceptance data and standards are unavailable.

Questions equipment owners ask

Can an out-of-production CDI still be evaluated?

Possibly. Serviceability depends on the exact platform, installed configuration, condition, documentation, access and parts availability. Send the machine designation and the symptom for a model-specific evaluation.

Which CDI families does this page cover?

CDI Spark, CDI Advance and CDI Crystal families are identified here. The exact service boundary is confirmed from the installed model and configuration rather than from the family name alone.

Do you install or license Esko workflow software?

No. Software-related work is limited to the customer’s licensed environment and customer-authorized access. Licensing, entitlement and manufacturer-controlled services remain with the software owner and Esko.

Are you authorized by Esko?

No. PhaseX is an independent service company, not an authorized Esko service center, agent or affiliate, and does not perform Esko warranty work.

What should we send first?

Send the machine model, serial number, installed options, the exact operator report, location, production impact and any available screen or plate images that you are authorized to share.
