TITANIUM and PLATINUM are two different service objects

TITANIUM is SPGPrints' multifunction computer-to-plate system, published in 25×30 and 48×35 inch variants and known in older installations as variLEX. PLATINUM is a direct laser engraver, formerly KRONOS, built around a sealed CO2 or fiber laser platform for sleeves, rollers and plate-on-drum work.

They are often spoken about as one “SPGPrints engraver”, and that is where service requests go wrong. A CTP imaging fault and a direct-engraving fault sit in different hardware, so the first thing we establish is which machine is in front of us and in which configuration.

Scope in one paragraph

PhaseX can evaluate drum and sleeve handling, chucks, mandrels and drive, rotary and linear motion with feedback, accessible external optics and laser interfaces, industrial controls and I/O, interlocks, cooling, vacuum and extraction. We do not open a sealed laser or engraving head, we do not supply or alter Active 3D RIP recipes or calibrated material databases, we do not install OEM software, licences or updates, and we do not accept a print form or release production. Those remain with the customer and SPGPrints.

Observable machine symptoms

  • Machine will not reach ready, or aborts partway through a job
  • Sleeve or roller will not clamp, or clamping force has changed
  • Engraving or imaging depth uneven along or around the form
  • Rotary or linear reference, registration or speed fault
  • Laser unavailable, unstable indication or communication loss
  • Cooling, flow, temperature or extraction alarm
  • Cycle completes but the form no longer matches the plant's reference

A symptom routes the evaluation; it does not diagnose a failed part.

Access, safety and customer-owned decisions

Send alarm screens, nameplates and the configuration page. Do not send Active 3D RIP recipes, calibrated material databases, licence keys or customer artwork.

Print-form acceptance, material selection and production release stay with the plant. OEM software, licences and updates stay with SPGPrints.

What to send before service planning

  • Whether the machine is a TITANIUM or a PLATINUM, and the exact model line
  • Serial number and, for older units, the previous variLEX or KRONOS badge
  • Laser type and installed source interface where visible
  • Sleeve, roller or plate handling configuration
  • Exact alarm text and the first observable change
  • Site access contact and available service window

Questions owners ask

Is PhaseX authorised or appointed by SPGPrints?

No. We hold no SPGPrints appointment, no OEM software access and no licence rights. We are an independent service company and we state that in writing before work starts.

Can you work on a machine still badged variLEX or KRONOS?

We can evaluate it on the same terms as the current badge. What decides the scope is the serial number, the installed configuration and the retrofit history, not the name on the door.

Will you open the sealed laser or engraving head?

No. We establish whether the fault sits in the sealed module or in the drum, handling, motion, control or utility chain around it, and we route an OEM-only module rather than opening it.

Can you restore or adjust an Active 3D RIP recipe?

No. RIP recipes, calibrated material databases and OEM software stay with the plant and SPGPrints. We report measured machine condition and work on machine hardware.
