What we are called to

The systems behind the machines

Five equipment families we are called out to, in the order their owners feel a stoppage. Each panel shows what the system is made of and where our responsibility starts and stops.

  1. Semiconductor and advanced packaging

    A microprocessing or advanced-packaging tool that stops holds up every wafer behind it in the line. We work the source interface, the beam delivery, the stage and motion, the control and interlocks, the extraction and the utilities. A sealed optical module is not opened: it is replaced, or routed back to its maker, and we tell you which before you commit.

    Laser source and subsystem repair
  2. Battery and e-mobility

    Busbar welding, can-to-cap sealing and tray lines run on fiber sources, programmable optics, fixtures and the sensor and vision interfaces that gate the weld. That is electronics, optics and control hardware, and it is what we evaluate and repair. Weld qualification and the production record stay with you.

    Industrial laser service
  3. Scientific and ultrafast laboratory systems

    An ultrafast system on an optical bench is not a machine tool and does not fail like one. Oscillator and seed, amplifier, drivers and power supplies, the optical path, thermal control and the measurement that proves the output: we work at component level on platforms whose support route has often lapsed.

    Scientific laser repair
  4. Packaging, plate imaging and coding

    A plate imager or a coding head holding up a converting line is a production emergency with a short fuse. Imaging source, scan head and optics, drum or web motion, registration and encoders, machine control and extraction are all in scope. Print and plate quality remain yours to judge.

    Packaging laser service
  5. Robotic welding, cladding and deposition

    Cladding and deposition cells add a powder or wire delivery path, a cladding head and shielding to the source, motion and control hardware we already work on. We inspect the system as a whole and set out repair, replacement or a routed partner in the written proposal. Metallurgical qualification of a deposit is yours.

    Retrofit and modernisation

What we do

What our laser technical services cover

01

Installation and commissioning

Machine placement coordination, utility checks, alignment, first cuts and operator handover for new and imported laser machinery: flatbed and tube cutters, robotic cells, coil-fed and automated loading lines, and laser welding, cleaning and cladding systems. Site readiness is reviewed before delivery.

02

Diagnostics and field repair

Machine-down service calls: cutting problems, motion and CNC faults, chiller and cooling issues, interlocks, electrical troubleshooting, and cutting-head work on Raytools, BOCI, OSPRI and similar heads.

03

Laser source and power systems

Component-level diagnostics and repair on Raycus, IPG, MAX, JPT and GW sources, power supplies and DC modules: the failures that usually mean waiting for an overseas technician.

04

Fiber delivery cable support

Inspection and damage assessment of high-power delivery cables and QBH/QD connections, with removal, managed repair and reinstallation handled under a defined scope.

05

Relocation and recommissioning

Pre-move condition documentation, dismantling scope, utility requirements at the new site, transport coordination with rigging partners, and startup after the move.

06

Retrofit and modernization

Conversion of legacy CO2 machines to fiber, and modernization of aging Trumpf, Amada and other European and Japanese platforms with current-generation sources, cutting heads and control systems. We have done these conversions before; scope and feasibility are defined per machine.

07

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled inspection and maintenance with written reports, so machine condition is documented before it becomes downtime.

What we service, and what sits outside it

PhaseX is an independent industrial laser service company working across the United States and Canada. Industrial laser repair on cutting, welding, marking and cleaning machines; fiber laser repair and laser source repair down to the driver, control and interface boards; packaging and printing laser machinery; and scientific and ultrafast laboratory systems. On site or on a bench, under a written scope and a fixed evaluation fee quoted before anyone travels.

Two different trades share the word laser and this company is neither of them: medical and aesthetic laser treatment for people is a clinical business, and office laser printer repair is an IT one. We do service laser cutting machines - the machine, the source, the head and the controls - but we do not sell cutting work itself, so if you need parts cut, a job shop is the right call.
