Notes on laser equipment and service

Short pieces on what the equipment actually is, and on describing a problem in a way that shortens the job. No product reviews, no repair procedures, and nothing here diagnoses a machine from a single symptom.

Home/Notes
  • The same flexo plate complaint, three different machines to look atFlexo platemaking

    A shallow or inconsistent plate can come from the imager, the exposure step or the processor, and which of those exist at all depends on the platemaking route you run.

    Read itFlexo plate imaging service

  • CO2, fiber and UV laser coders: is it a marking problem or an inspection problem?Coding and marking

    A code that a person can read and a code the vision system accepts are two different observations. Separating them, and naming the laser family and the substrate, is what makes a coder service request useful.

    Read itPackaging coding and marking service

  • Machine, laser source, controller: three identities on one converting lineConverting and finishing

    The name on a converting machine, the laser source inside it and the controller on its operator panel are three separate pieces of information. Knowing which one you are looking at shortens a service request.

    Read itLaser die cutting and converting service

  • A sealed ultrafast laser is not an unrepairable oneUltrafast systems

    Low output from an ultrafast system says little about which part failed, and a closed enclosure says nothing about whether it can be repaired. What helps is identifying the installed architecture.

    Read itUltrafast laser service
