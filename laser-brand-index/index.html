Why this index exists

PhaseX Laser Services is an independent laser service company working across the United States and Canada. We are not an authorized service centre, agent or affiliate of any manufacturer named on this page, and we do not perform manufacturer warranty work. Brand and model names identify equipment only and remain the property of their owners.

That independence is the reason to call us rather than a reservation about doing so. An independent company can look at a platform whose maker has moved on, quote component-level work where the only factory route is a replacement, and give you a written scope and a fixed evaluation fee before anyone travels. What we will not do is tell you what is wrong with your machine before we have looked at it.

How to use this index

If you know the brand, find it in its equipment class below. If you know only what the machine is doing, start from the symptom index instead - that is the faster route when the label is not the question. If the machine was imported and local support has thinned out, the imported fiber laser machine page is written for exactly that situation.

Three of these brands have their own page, because the service story genuinely differs rather than because the name differs: Raycus, Han's Laser and Esko CDI. Everything else is answered by the class page it belongs to. We have deliberately not built a page per brand: a hundred pages that differ only in a name help nobody and are the oldest bad habit in this industry.

Where the product-family names come from

A product family appears here only when it was read from the manufacturer's own domain - a product page, a datasheet or an operation manual on that manufacturer's site - and the URL and the date are recorded in our source ledger. Distributor catalogues and spec-aggregator sites were not used to fill a gap.

Where a designation named in our own working inventory could not be confirmed that way, it is not printed. Four were dropped on that basis in this round, and the ledger records what was looked for and not found, so a later round cannot quietly reintroduce them. For those platforms the entry asks for the nameplate instead - which is what we would ask on the phone anyway.

Questions owners ask

Do you service brands that are not on this list?

Yes. This index covers the equipment we see most often in North America; it is not a limit. Tell us the manufacturer and the designation on the label and we will tell you whether it is something we can take on.

Are you an authorized service provider for these manufacturers?

No. PhaseX is an independent service company and is not an authorized service centre, agent, representative or affiliate of any manufacturer named here, and does not perform manufacturer warranty work. Where equipment is under an active manufacturer warranty, we work only within a scope the equipment seller or manufacturer has authorized in writing.

Will working with an independent company void my warranty?

That depends on your warranty terms and on who holds them, which is why we ask about warranty status before quoting rather than after. If your unit is in warranty and the work would fall outside what the seller has authorized, we will say so and tell you to go to them first.

Why do some entries name product families and others ask for the nameplate?

Because a family is printed here only when we read it on the manufacturer's own site. Where the manufacturer publishes no current page for a series - which is common and is often the oldest equipment still in service - we would rather ask you for the label than print a designation we cannot stand behind.

What does an evaluation cost and what do I get?

A fixed fee, quoted in writing before anyone travels, and an assessment of what can be established about the equipment. If site or bench work makes sense after that, you get a written scope and a fixed price for your approval. If it does not, you get that answer in writing too.
