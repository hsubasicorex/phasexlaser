PHASEX LASER SERVICES
FIELD SERVICE REPORT -- BLANK TEMPLATE

This is the blank template of the report an equipment manufacturer, distributor
or importer receives after each field visit. It is published so you can see the
deliverable before you engage anyone.

What this template is not: it is not a procedure, and it contains no settings,
parameter values, measurement points, test order or calibration method. Those
belong to the engineer and to the service documentation you supply, and they are
not published. The template describes what you receive, not how the work is done.

This template is published in English only. The page that links to it is
available in English, French, Spanish and German, and a report can be delivered
in English on any engagement; ask about other languages when the scope is agreed.

PhaseX Laser Services is an independent service provider and is not affiliated
with, endorsed by or authorized by any manufacturer. Manufacturer, brand and
model names are used only to identify equipment.
