LPBF, DMP and multi-laser production platforms

LPBF machines combine one or more lasers and scanners with a recoater, build platform motion, controlled gas flow, filtration, cooling, industrial controls and a safety chain. A build interruption can begin in any one of those layers.

PhaseX evaluates the accessible machine subsystem and documents what can be established. The service boundary is deliberately narrower than the machine: proprietary optics, calibrated scan-field geometry, licensed parameter software and validated material recipes remain with the machine builder and owner.

Scope in one paragraph

PhaseX can evaluate accessible recoater and motion hardware, build-platform drives and feedback, gas-flow and filter interfaces, cooling, vacuum/purge hardware, controls, communications, I/O and safety chains, plus accessible source and scanner interfaces where authorization and architecture permit. We do not open sealed optical modules, perform OEM scanner calibration, modify licensed build parameters, qualify a material/process, or certify a printed part. Powder control, material lots, build qualification and part acceptance remain with the owner.

Observable machine symptoms

  • Laser or scanner unavailable, reference fault or communication loss
  • Recoater, build-platform or axis interruption
  • Gas-flow, oxygen, filter, pressure or purge alarm
  • Cooling, flow or temperature trip
  • Intermittent build abort, control reboot or I/O fault
  • Door, inerting or safety chain that will not reach the machine-ready state

A symptom routes the evaluation; it does not diagnose a failed part.

Access, safety and customer-owned decisions

Before field work, the customer confirms in writing that powder has been removed or contained as required, the chamber and work area are in a safe state, purge and inert-gas conditions are understood, and site EHS requirements have been communicated. PhaseX does not take custody of metal powder or choose its handling method.

Build parameters, material release, powder-lot disposition, process qualification and acceptance of aerospace, medical or other certified parts remain with the owner and its approving authority. A PhaseX machine report is not a build or part certificate.

What to send before service planning

  • Builder, exact model, serial number, machine generation and laser count
  • Material last run and written powder/chamber readiness status
  • Exact alarm text, event time and the first subsystem to change
  • Recoater, gas, filter, cooling and peripheral configuration
  • Authorized photographs of labels, HMI alarms and machine status
  • Site EHS contact and the person responsible for build qualification

Questions owners ask

What must be completed before PhaseX enters an LPBF machine area?

The customer must confirm in writing that powder is removed or contained as its procedure requires, the chamber and work area are safe, purge and inert-gas conditions are understood, and site EHS requirements have been communicated.

Can PhaseX service the laser and scanner on an LPBF platform?

We can evaluate accessible power, communication, cooling and machine-side source or scanner interfaces where the architecture and authorization permit. We do not open sealed optical modules or perform proprietary OEM scan-field calibration.

Will a PhaseX report qualify a build, material or printed part?

No. Licensed build parameters, material and powder-lot disposition, process qualification and acceptance of aerospace, medical or other certified parts remain with the owner and its approving authority.

What should an owner send before service planning?

Send the exact machine model and generation, serial number, laser count, last material, written powder and chamber status, exact alarms, and the recoater, gas, filter, cooling and peripheral configuration.
