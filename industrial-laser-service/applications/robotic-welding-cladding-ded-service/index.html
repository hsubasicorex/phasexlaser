What a deposition cell is, as a service object

Laser material deposition is in wide industrial use for repairing turbomachinery components and for coating rollers and cylinders: the laser forms a melt pool and metal is fed into it, layer by layer, along a controlled path. Coaxial wire heads exist alongside powder ones, and high-speed variants are used as an alternative to hard chrome.

As a machine, that is a high-power source, beam delivery to a cladding or welding head, a powder or wire delivery path with its own mechanism, a robot or motion system, shielding gas, and a cell controller with its safety chain. The head is not a cutting head and the powder feeder is not a wire feeder; they are separate mechanisms with separate failure behaviour, and a proposal that treats them as one is not worth reading.

What gets reported

The deposit is the signal. These are the forms it takes:

  • inconsistent bead, or a bead that changes along the path
  • poor fusion, or a result the shop no longer trusts
  • powder feed that surges, blocks or runs lean
  • wire feed that stalls or births a bird nest
  • a head that overheats, or whose optics foul faster than they used to
  • shielding that no longer protects the pool
  • the robot or motion path faulting mid-programme
A deposit complaint is a system complaint. It is the reason we ask to see the cell rather than the component somebody has already decided is guilty.

What is in scope, and what is not

In scope: the high-power source with its power supplies, driver and control electronics and thermal hardware; beam delivery and the cladding or welding head with its optics and cooling; the powder or wire delivery hardware; the robot or motion system and its controller; shielding gas delivery; the cell controller, interlocks and safety chain; extraction around the cell.

The powder or wire delivery path is evaluated against the configuration in front of us, and whether it is repaired, replaced or routed to a partner is settled in the written proposal after the inspection - not promised before it.

Not in scope: metallurgical and dimensional qualification of a deposited layer. That is the customer’s, and where the component is a certified part it belongs to the certificate holder and the regulator. We service the cell. We never sign for the part.

How a job runs

Fixed evaluation fee, quoted before travel. The cell is recorded as found, including the feed mechanism and its condition, and you receive a written report with the repair, replace or route decision set out per subsystem and the commercial options against each.

What to send with a request

Enough to answer you properly, and nothing that needs the machine opened.

  • the cell builder and model, and the head and feeder make if they differ
  • powder or wire, and what the cell is normally depositing
  • what the deposit looks like now against what it looked like before
  • when it changed and what changed first
  • the fault text or code exactly as shown
  • photographs of the head, the feed path, the shielding arrangement and the cell

Questions owners ask

Do you service the powder feeder, or only the laser side?

Both are in scope. The powder or wire delivery path is a separate mechanism from the source and the head, and we treat it as one: it is evaluated against the configuration in front of us, and the written proposal states whether we repair it, replace it, or route it to a partner better placed to. What we do not do is promise an answer before the inspection.

Can you tell us why our deposit is failing?

We can tell you the state of the equipment that produced it, in writing. Where the cause is in the hardware, that is the answer. What we cannot do is qualify the deposit itself - the metallurgical and dimensional judgement is yours, against your own specification and records.

Our cell repairs turbine components. Does that change anything?

It changes who owns the outcome, and it is worth being explicit about. Airworthiness, part certification and process qualification sit with the certificate holder and the regulator. We are not a repair station and we hold no approval. We service the machine, never the part.

Is cladding just welding with extra steps?

Not for service purposes. A cladding head, a powder path and closed-loop monitoring are mechanisms a welding cell does not have, and they fail in ways a welding cell does not. Treating them as the same thing is how a quote comes back wrong.
