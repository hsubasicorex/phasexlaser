Stent, hypotube, guidewire and catheter production systems

These machines combine a laser with fine rotary and linear motion, tube handling, process viewing, extraction and a safety-controlled enclosure. A production interruption may be reported as a laser fault even when the interruption is in the collet, tube feed, encoder, vision chain, cooling loop or machine control.

PhaseX accepts complete-machine evaluation when the owner can provide authorized access and the system can be made safe for service. We identify the affected machine layer before proposing repair, replacement or OEM routing.

Scope in one paragraph

PhaseX can evaluate accessible source and fiber interfaces, external beam-delivery hardware, rotary and linear motion, collets and feeders, machine vision, industrial controls, interlocks, cooling and extraction. We do not open sealed proprietary optical modules, supply or alter validated process recipes, approve medical-device process capability, or release product. Customer change control, requalification and acceptance of every manufactured part remain with the equipment owner.

Observable machine symptoms

  • No start, intermittent abort or a safety chain that will not reset
  • Rotary axis, collet, tube-feed or reference-position fault
  • Vision image, alignment or part-presence fault
  • Laser/source unavailable, unstable output indication or communication loss
  • Cooling, flow, temperature, extraction or enclosure alarm
  • Cycle completes but repeatability, cut location or handling has changed

A symptom routes the evaluation; it does not diagnose a failed part.

Access, safety and customer-owned decisions

Do not send a released production recipe, password, patient information or controlled product record. Alarm screens, machine labels and the configuration page are normally enough to start.

After machine work, the owner decides what requalification, capability study, inspection and change-control record is required before production resumes. PhaseX reports machine findings; it does not approve a medical device or manufacturing process.

What to send before service planning

  • Machine builder, model, serial number and year if shown
  • Installed laser/source label and controller/HMI generation
  • Tube loader, feeder, rotary/chuck and vision configuration
  • Exact alarm text and the first observable change
  • Whether production is stopped, degraded or under planned maintenance
  • Site access, decontamination and customer change-control contact

Questions owners ask

What can PhaseX evaluate without changing a validated medical process?

We evaluate accessible machine layers: motion, tube handling, vision, controls, interlocks, cooling, extraction and permitted source or fiber interfaces. We do not alter validated recipes or make a product-release decision.

Will PhaseX open a sealed laser or proprietary optical module?

No. A sealed proprietary module is not opened. We establish whether the fault is in that module or in the surrounding machine and route an OEM-only module accordingly.

Who decides whether the line must be requalified after service?

The equipment owner and its quality function decide the change-control, requalification, capability study, inspection and product-release requirements. A PhaseX report documents machine work; it is not a medical-device release.

What information is needed before service planning?

Send the machine model and serial number, source label, controller generation, tube-handling and vision configuration, exact alarm text, first observable change and the site change-control contact. Do not send patient data or released production recipes.
