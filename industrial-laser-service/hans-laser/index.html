One name, three kinds of equipment

Han's Laser Technology Industry Group is a group rather than a single product line, and it builds across the field. In North America the name turns up in three distinct places, and the service conversation is different in each.

On a laser source. The questions are source questions - output, delivery, control and interface - and the page that answers them is laser source repair.

On a complete cutting machine. Here the name is on the frame, and the source and head inside it may be from the same group or from somebody else entirely. That is the imported-machine situation, and the three-manufacturer split is covered on our imported fiber laser machine page.

On a marking or coding system in a production line. Here the machine is part of a line, the failure usually surfaces as a code quality or verification problem rather than as a laser problem, and the page that covers it is packaging coding and marking.

So the first thing we will ask is which of those three you have. Tell us in your first message and the reply is useful immediately.

The support situation, said without drama

Han's is a very large manufacturer with a real international presence, which makes this a different situation from a machine whose importer has disappeared. What owners here run into instead is distance: response times measured against a production schedule, a support route that runs through a regional office, and older platforms that have moved down the priority list.

That is a reasonable thing for a large manufacturer to do and a bad thing to be on the receiving end of when a line is stopped. An independent company is useful here not because it knows the platform better than its maker, but because it can be on site, look at the whole machine including the parts the manufacturer did not build, and put a written scope and a fixed fee in front of you this week.

What we accept

Han's equipment is accepted for evaluation and service in all three of the forms above - source, complete machine, and marking or coding system in a line - on site across the United States and Canada, or on a bench where a subassembly is better looked at off the machine. Including platforms out of production and machines bought second-hand with no documented history.

Questions owners ask

The label says Han's but I do not know whether that is the machine or the laser.

Both are possible, and that is the normal confusion with this brand. Photograph the plate on the machine frame and the label on the laser source separately. On most machines those are two different manufacturers; on this brand they are often both this one.

Do you service Han's marking systems in packaging lines as well as cutting machines?

Yes, and it is a genuinely different kind of job. A marking system in a line usually fails as a code-quality or verification problem rather than as a dead laser, and it is covered on our packaging coding and marking page.

Han's has a presence in North America. Why call an independent company?

Sometimes you should not - if the unit is in warranty, go to them. What an independent company offers is a written scope and a fixed fee now, on the whole machine rather than only the parts they built, and without a commercial interest in selling you the next machine.

Can you support an older platform the manufacturer has moved on from?

That is a large part of what we do across every brand on this site. Send the designation from the label and we will tell you what can be established about it.
